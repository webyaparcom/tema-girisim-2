Smart hive · Mentese, Mugla

Listen to the bees without opening the hive

Kovan Sensor is a scale that sits under the hive plus a temperature, humidity and sound sensor on the lid. You see the nectar flow, swarming signs and the winter cluster on your phone. Fictional demo content; images are representative.

Inside the sensor

Six cells, one hive

Each comb cell describes one job of the sensor.

  • scale

    01

    Scale

    Daily weight change of the hive; an alert when the nectar flow starts.

  • device_thermostat

    02

    Temperature

    Brood-nest temperature; the winter cluster is tracked.

  • water_drop

    03

    Humidity

    An alert threshold for condensation risk.

  • graphic_eq

    04

    Sound

    A change in humming can signal swarm preparation.

  • solar_power

    05

    Solar panel

    A small panel and battery; no charging needed all season.

  • cell_tower

    06

    Remote apiary

    A low-power network; data arrives even on hillsides without coverage.
